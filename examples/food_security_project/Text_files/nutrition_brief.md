# Nutrition *brief*

Child **malnutrition** remains high, and dietary diversity is low.[^1] Stunting affects one child in three.

Key findings
------------

- Diets rely on maize and rice, with few vegetables.
- Clean water and sanitation are lacking; diarrhoea is common.
  Hygiene campaigns reached few villages.
1. Micronutrient supplements reduced anaemia.
2. School meals improved nutrition and attendance.

Undernutrition rates rose between 2019 and
2020. Since then they have stabilised.

| Indicator | 2022 | 2023 |
|-----------|------|------|
| Stunting (%) | 34 | 33 |
| Food price index | 120 | 145 |

The IPC Phase 3 classification now covers two districts, a first in the 21<sup>st</sup> century.

[^1]: Based on a 2023 household survey of 1,200 families.
