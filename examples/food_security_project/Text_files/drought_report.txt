DROUGHT UPDATE
The 2023 drought reduced the maize harvest by a third.The rice crop also declined in the north.
Livestock losses were reported in three districts... Farmers sold animals early.
Food prices rose very sharply after the drought.[4] Many households cannot afford a basic diet.
See https://example.org/report for details.

El Niño is expected to bring erratic rains again next season. The lean season may start early.
